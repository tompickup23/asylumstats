**1,087 people returned to France. 27,920 arrived by small boat.** Between 6 August 2025, when the UK-France agreement began operating, and 30 June 2026, the Home Office returned 1,087 people to France and accepted 1,117 transferred to the UK in exchange. Over exactly the same dates, 27,920 people were detected crossing the Channel in small boats.

That is a return for about **3.9%** of arrivals. The Home Office does not publish that ratio: it is our calculation, dividing its published returns by the sum of its own daily small boats series over the same dates.

**Returns rose, unevenly.** The scheme started slowly, dipped in December and February, and picked up from March. Setting each month's returns against that month's arrivals gives a sense of scale, not the fate of that month's arrivals, since a person returned in June may have crossed earlier.

| Month | Returned to France | Small boat arrivals | Returns as a share |
|---|---|---|---|
| September 2025 | 17 | 5,084 | 0.3% |
| December 2025 | 64 | 2,180 | 2.9% |
| March 2026 | 109 | 2,232 | 4.9% |
| April 2026 | 142 | 1,975 | 7.2% |
| May 2026 | 218 | 2,726 | 8.0% |
| June 2026 | 264 | 2,742 | 9.6% |

**The agreement has ended.** It was extended from June by an exchange of letters between the two governments, which came into force on 9 June 2026 and ran "until 1 October 2026". No further extension was published. On 3 November 2026 the Home Office told the House of Lords: "The UK-France Treaty has ended and there are no current plans for the scheme to resume once operational activity has concluded." The borders inspector's report, published on 10 November 2026, records that French officials said in May 2026 that France would not agree to an extension beyond 1 October.

**The only later total is a rounded one.** On 19 October 2026 the Home Office said in a written answer: "Over 1,500 individuals have been returned to France since the pilot began with a similar number transferred to the UK". That is a count of people returned over the whole pilot, with no end date given and no exact figure, so it sits beside the 1,087 to 30 June 2026 and does not replace it. The month by month figures above stop at June because nothing later has been published.

**What this does not show.** Returns under the agreement are one route out among several: the Home Office also removes and deports people by other means, counted in its quarterly returns statistics. And because the UK takes one person from France for each one returned, the agreement is an exchange rather than a reduction in numbers.
