**Every ethnic projection is uncertain.** The question is how uncertain.

We ran 1,000 Monte Carlo simulations, perturbing each of the 585,144 Census-derived cohort change ratios to produce a probability distribution of future ethnic composition for every local authority.

The result: for most areas, the **80% confidence interval is ±3 percentage points** at 2051 and ±5pp at 2061. This means if we project Burnley at 39% White British in 2051, we're 80% confident it falls between 36% and 42%.

No other published UK ethnic projection provides statistical confidence intervals. Goodwin's CHSS report shows a single trajectory. NEWETHPOP published two scenarios. We show the full probability distribution.

**Data**: 1,000 Monte Carlo simulations, CCR perturbation σ=0.04 calibrated from NEWETHPOP backcast validation (MAE 3.94pp across 299 local authorities). Following Yu, Sevcikova, Raftery & Curran (2023, *Demography*).
