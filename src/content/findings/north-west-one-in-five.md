**19,425 people on asylum support in the North West at 30 September 2026. One in five nationally.**

The region carries **20.8%** of the 93,453 people on asylum support in the UK. The West Midlands is second on 11,748. London is third on 11,122, or **11.9%**.

| Region or nation | On asylum support | Share |
|---|---|---|
| North West | 19,425 | 20.8% |
| West Midlands | 11,748 | 12.6% |
| London | 11,122 | 11.9% |
| East Midlands | 8,233 | 8.8% |
| Scotland | 7,627 | 8.2% |
| Yorkshire and The Humber | 7,605 | 8.1% |
| North East | 7,083 | 7.6% |
| South East | 6,479 | 6.9% |
| East of England | 4,968 | 5.3% |
| South West | 3,418 | 3.7% |
| Wales | 3,386 | 3.6% |
| Northern Ireland | 2,359 | 2.5% |

Per head of population the North East is slightly higher: 25.6 people on asylum support per 10,000 residents against 25.0 in the North West.

**London held more three years ago.** At 30 September 2023, the peak of the supported population, London had 24,383 people on support and the North West 22,379. By 30 September 2024 the North West held more, and the gap has widened at each September since.

| At 30 September | North West | Share | London | Share | UK |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 22,379 | 18.1% | 24,383 | 19.7% | 123,758 |
| 2024 | 21,084 | 19.3% | 19,922 | 18.3% | 109,024 |
| 2025 | 21,684 | 19.4% | 18,127 | 16.2% | 111,651 |
| 2026 | 19,425 | 20.8% | 11,122 | 11.9% | 93,453 |

The UK total fell by 30,305 over those three years. London fell by 13,261 and the North West by 2,954. The system has shrunk much faster in London than in the North West. The region by region change in the latest year is in [Asylum support fell 39% in London in a year. In Scotland it rose 20%.](/findings/asylum-support-regional-shift-sep-2026/)

**Updated 27 November 2026.** This page previously gave the position at 30 June 2026: 18,962 in the North West, 20.3% of 93,293, with London second on 12,140. It now uses the Home Office figures as at 30 September 2026, published on 26 November 2026, and adds the three-year comparison. The 2023 shares are of a UK total that includes 84 people the Home Office assigned to no region, and the 2024 shares of one that includes 2,843.

**Why here.** The prime accommodation provider for the North West is Serco, on a contract that runs to 2029. One company handles placement across the whole region. The Home Office contract decides where people go, and the council is told rather than asked.

Within the region the load is not spread evenly either. Four Lancashire authorities are among the ten highest in the country once you count per head of population, and none of them is Manchester or Liverpool.
