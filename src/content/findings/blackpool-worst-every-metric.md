**Correction, 31 August 2026.** This page previously said Blackpool was worst or near-worst on every domain, and gave its asylum figure as 577 people at 40.0 per 10,000. That was the position at 31 December 2025. At 30 June 2026 Blackpool has **236 people on asylum support at 16.5 per 10,000**. Blackpool's own place page was already showing the newer figure while this page showed the older one.

**The four rows are not the same vintage**: crime is the year ending March 2026, social care 2024-25, SEND the 2025/26 school year and the asylum figure 30 June 2026.

The deprivation argument holds for the two domains where Blackpool is near the top. Blackpool remains the most deprived local authority in England on IMD 2019, and recorded crime and social care spend both follow deprivation rather than dispersal policy. Asylum accommodation has moved out of Blackpool faster than the national average: 16,021 people were in hotel accommodation across the UK at 30 June 2026, against 30,657 six months earlier.
