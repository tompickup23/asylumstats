Counted per head of population, four East Lancashire authorities sit in the **top ten of 361** UK local authorities for people on asylum support, at 30 September 2026.

| Rank by rate | Council | On support | Per 10,000 people |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Glasgow City | 4,114 | 62.9 |
| 2 | **Hyndburn** | 424 | 48.8 |
| 3 | Braintree | 784 | 47.1 |
| 4 | Belfast | 1,653 | 47.0 |
| 5 | **Blackburn with Darwen** | 767 | 46.8 |
| 6 | Halton | 609 | 46.2 |
| 7 | Coventry | 1,677 | 46.0 |
| 8 | Middlesbrough | 700 | 45.8 |
| 9 | **Burnley** | 461 | 45.7 |
| 10 | **Pendle** | 451 | 45.0 |

**Two rankings, two different answers.** Ranked by the raw count, Burnley is 63rd of 361 and Hyndburn 71st, which is how they appear on their own place pages and in most reporting. Ranked per head of population they are 9th and 2nd. Neither ranking is wrong. They answer different questions, and only the second one tells you what a place is carrying relative to its size.

Three of the ten largest caseloads are also in the top ten by rate: Glasgow City, Belfast and Coventry. The other seven places in this table are smaller councils. The largest caseloads are mostly in large cities. Most of the heaviest loads are in towns.

**For comparison, elsewhere in Lancashire:** Preston is 46th by rate on 24.9 per 10,000, Blackpool 89th on 16.5, and Ribble Valley 212th on 4.8. The four authorities above are not a Lancashire-wide pattern. They are an East Lancashire one.

**Updated and corrected, 27 November 2026.** This page was headlined "Four Lancashire authorities are in the national top nine", on the position at 30 June 2026, when Hyndburn was 2nd, Burnley 4th, Blackburn with Darwen 6th and Pendle 9th. On the Home Office figures as at 30 September 2026, published on 26 November 2026, Pendle is 10th, so the four are in the top ten. The page also said that nowhere in the top ten by count appeared in the top nine by rate. That was wrong when written: Glasgow City and Coventry were in both lists, as the page's own table showed.

The prime accommodation provider across the whole region is Serco, on a contract running to 2029.
