Across all **25 local authorities** in our ethnic projection dataset, the White British share of population fell in every single one between the 2011 and 2021 Census.

The decline ranges from **-0.97pp/year** (Blackburn) to **-0.09pp/year** (Wyre), but no council recorded stability or growth.

The fastest-declining areas — Blackburn, Pendle, Preston — are also among the highest asylum dispersal areas in the North West. This creates a compounding effect: rapid demographic change from long-term migration patterns, overlaid with short-term asylum dispersal.

Whether this correlation reflects policy design or coincidence is a question the Home Office dispersal model should answer transparently.
