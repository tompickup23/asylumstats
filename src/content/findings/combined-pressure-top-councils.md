> **Correction, 12 October 2026.** This page was headlined "Recomputed: Blackburn,
> Blackpool and Pendle carry the highest combined pressure". Three of the index's
> five domains, police recorded crime, adult social care spend and SEND growth,
> came from 25-authority files typed into this site's source by hand in April
> 2026, with no raw file or script behind them, and they could not be traced to
> ONS, NHS Digital or DfE tables. Those files gave adult social care and SEND
> figures for district councils, which run neither service, and filed Preston,
> Staffordshire and Worcestershire under the wrong area codes. The table this page
> printed, Blackburn with Darwen **83.4**, Blackpool **79.9**, Pendle **78.9**,
> Doncaster **76.6**, Burnley **75.7**, Hyndburn **75.1** and Preston **75.0**,
> ranked only those 25 areas and is withdrawn. The three domains now come from
> the published tables, for every authority: ONS police recorded crime for the
> year ending March 2026, DfE pupils with an EHC plan for 2025/26 and DHSC adult
> social care spend for 2024-25. Recomputed across the 278 English authorities that
> hold all five domains, Manchester is first on **89.4**, Liverpool second on
> **88.0** and Middlesbrough third on **86.7**. Of the seven councils this page
> named, Burnley is sixth, Preston tenth, Blackburn with Darwen twelfth, Hyndburn
> sixteenth, Pendle eighteenth, Blackpool twenty-seventh and Doncaster
> thirty-ninth.

The composite index combines five domains at local authority level. Each is converted to a percentile rank against every authority holding that dataset, then averaged across the domains an area has:

1. Asylum support rate per 10,000 residents, against all 361 UK local authorities (Home Office local authority data, 30 June 2026)
2. Demographic change, the absolute annual percentage-point move in White British share between the 2011 and 2021 Censuses
3. Police recorded crime per 1,000 residents, excluding fraud, year ending March 2026 (ONS Police Force Area data tables, Table C2, on ONS mid-2025 population estimates)
4. Five-year growth in pupils with an EHC plan, 2020/21 to 2025/26 (DfE, Special educational needs in England)
5. Adult social care gross spend per resident aged 18 and over, 2024-25 (DHSC, Adult social care finance report, Table 14)

SEND and adult social care are run by upper-tier councils, so a two-tier district takes its county's figure and its county's percentile, and those two distributions hold one figure per council that runs the service.

Columns after the score are percentile ranks, where 100 is the most pressured. Burnley's social care and SEND columns are Lancashire County Council's.

**Caveats.** Authorities in Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland score on fewer domains, because the SEND and social care figures cover England, and they are not ranked here. The domains are different vintages: crime is the year ending March 2026, SEND the 2025/26 school year, social care 2024-25, the asylum figure 30 June 2026 and the demographic rate the Censuses of 2011 and 2021. The ranking will move whenever any of the five publishes, which is the point of dating it.

This index measures the co-occurrence of pressures on councils and public services. It does not claim that any one domain causes another.
