Blackburn with Darwen's White British population fell from **66.5%** in 2011 to **56.9%** in 2021 — a drop of **9.67 percentage points** in a single decade.

At the same annual rate of decline (-0.97pp/year), Blackburn is projected to fall below 50% White British by **2028** — making it one of the fastest-shifting local authorities in England.

The Asian population grew from 28.1% to 35.7% over the same period (+0.75pp/year).

By 2050, if trends continue, White British residents would represent just **28.8%** of Blackburn's population.

This is not a prediction. It is a linear extrapolation of ONS Census data. Real demographic change is shaped by migration policy, birth rates, and internal movement — all of which could accelerate or slow the trend.
