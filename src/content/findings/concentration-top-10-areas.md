**18,950 of the 93,453 people on asylum support** at 30 September 2026 were in ten local authorities. That is **20.3%** of the UK total in ten places out of 361.

| Council | On asylum support |
|---|---|
| Glasgow City | 4,114 |
| Birmingham | 2,158 |
| Liverpool | 2,071 |
| Manchester | 1,715 |
| Coventry | 1,677 |
| Belfast | 1,653 |
| Sandwell | 1,514 |
| Cardiff | 1,379 |
| Leicester | 1,339 |
| Nottingham | 1,330 |

At the other end, 14 of the 361 councils have nobody on asylum support.

**The total has fallen. The concentration has barely moved.** The supported population peaked at 123,758 at 30 September 2023, when the ten largest councils held 26,358 people, 21.3%. At 31 December 2025 they held 21,205 of 107,003, or 19.8%. They now hold 20.3%. The system is a quarter smaller than at its peak and about as concentrated.

The names have changed more than the share. At the 2023 peak the ten largest included Hillingdon, Hounslow, Barnet and Bradford. None is in the ten now. Sandwell, Cardiff, Leicester and Nottingham have replaced them. The regional pattern behind that is in [Asylum support fell 39% in London in a year. In Scotland it rose 20%.](/findings/asylum-support-regional-shift-sep-2026/)

Counted per head of population the table looks different again. Glasgow City, Belfast and Coventry are in both lists, and the other seven places in the per head top ten are much smaller councils. That list is in [Four Lancashire authorities are in the national top ten](/findings/lancashire-rate-table-top-nine/).

**Updated 27 November 2026.** This page previously gave the position at 30 June 2026: 18,301 of 93,293, or 19.6%. It now uses the Home Office figures as at 30 September 2026, published on 26 November 2026. Two statements have been corrected. The page said dozens of councils had nobody on support; the number was 15 at 30 June 2026 and is 14 now. It also said none of the ten largest councils was at the top of the per head list, when Glasgow City is first on both.

Source: Home Office, Immigration system statistics, [regional and local authority data](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/immigration-system-statistics-regional-and-local-authority-data), table Reg_02, editions as at 30 September 2023, 31 December 2025 and 30 September 2026.
